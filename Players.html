Play off performances by top players.

Harper had an outstanding freshman season at Rutgers and quickly established himself as one of the best players in college basketball. The 6-foot-6 guard averaged 19.4 points, 4.6 rebounds, and 4.2 assists per game while showcasing his scoring ability, court vision, and leadership. Harper earned National Player of the Week honors during the season and set Rutgers' freshman scoring record. Despite Rutgers finishing with a losing record, Harper consistently delivered big performances, including multiple 30-point games, and solidified himself as one of the top prospects in the 2025 NBA Draft. His combination of size, skill, and basketball IQ made him one of the most exciting freshmen in the nation.

Had a strong regular season in his rookie year with the San Antonio Spurs. The former Rutgers star averaged 11.8 points, 3.9 assists, and 3.4 rebounds in 69 games while shooting an efficient 50.5% from the field. Harper provided scoring, playmaking, and energy off the bench, helping the Spurs become one of the top teams in the NBA. His maturity, ability to attack the basket, and impact on winning earned him a spot on the NBA All-Rookie First Team and established him as one of the league's most promising young players.

He had a major role in the Spurs' 2026 playoff run to the NBA Finals. Throughout the postseason, the rookie guard provided consistent scoring, playmaking, and toughness against some of the league's best defenses.

Harper's ability to perform in big moments and his strong playoff performances established him as one of the league's most promising young players. He averaged 12.5 points, 4.2 assists, and 3.8 rebounds in the playoffs while shooting 48.7% from the field, showing that he can contribute at a high level in the most important games.
