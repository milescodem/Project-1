    Top Rookie Impresses Fans

    Dylan Harper exceeded expectations in his rookie season with the Spurs. He averaged 11.8 points and 3.9 assists while shooting efficiently and playing a key role on a Finals team. Harper's strong playoff performances and confidence in big moments established him as one of the league's most promising young players also.Cooper Flagg had one of the best rookie seasons in recent NBA history with the Dallas Mavericks. The No. 1 overall pick averaged 21.0 points, 6.7 rebounds, and 4.5 assists per game while starting all 70 games he played. Flagg's scoring, playmaking, and defensive ability helped him win the 2025–26 NBA Rookie of the Year award. He became only the second-youngest player ever to win the award and joined elite company by averaging at least 20 points, 6 rebounds, and 4 assists as a rookie. His outstanding first season showed why many consider him one of the NBA's future superstars.

    Finals MVP

    Jalen Brunson delivered an MVP-caliber season for the New York Knicks in 2025–26, leading the team to its first NBA championship in 53 years. Brunson was the heart of the Knicks' offense, providing elite scoring, playmaking, and leadership throughout the season and playoffs. He earned Eastern Conference Finals MVP and NBA Finals MVP honors, including a dominant 45-point performance in the championship-clinching game against the Spurs. His clutch play, consistency, and ability to perform in the biggest moments made him one of the most valuable players in the league and a Knicks legend.
