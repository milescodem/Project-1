Break Down

SAN ANTONIO (AP) — Jalen Brunson was the star of Game 1 of the NBA Finals, willing the New York Knicks to a series-opening win. Victor Wembanyama, even while struggling from the field, put up big numbers for the San Antonio Spurs in his finals debut as well. The stars were stars.

Expectations

The Knicks enter with momentum after taking Game 1 and will try to put pressure on San Antonio by going up 2–0 before the series shifts to New York. The Spurs are likely to play with more urgency, especially on defense and rebounding, after struggling offensively in the opener. Expect a slower, physical game. Finals games often become more defensive as teams adjust to each other's strengths. A key factor will be whether San Antonio can get more scoring support around its stars and avoid turnovers. For New York, the goal is to keep controlling the pace, defend well, and continue getting balanced scoring from multiple players.

X-Factor

  • O.G Anunoby
  • Karl-Anthony Towns
  • Dylan Harper
  • Deaaron Fox

Prediction

A close game that could go either way, with the Knicks having a slight edge because of their confidence from Game 1, but the Spurs should be much tougher at home in a must-win situation.
